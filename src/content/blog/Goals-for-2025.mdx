\*\* Learning a new Front-end framework \*\*

I've been deep in React-land for a while now, but I'm itching to explore other frameworks. Sure, they might all be heading in similar directions these days, but I want to get my hands dirty and really understand what makes each one tick.

My plan is to build something substantial in each framework - not just toy projects, but real applications that push me to understand their philosophies and trade-offs. It's not just about adding tools to my belt; it's about developing a more nuanced perspective on when each framework shines (or doesn't). With that being said, below are the 3 on my watchlist:

1. Vue
2. Angular
3. Svelte
